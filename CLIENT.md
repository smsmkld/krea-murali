## What we are selling
The **Pre-LOI Review**: a confidential read of a buyer's CIM before they
sign an LOI.
- Delivered within **5 business days** of receiving the CIM
- Includes: fit check against their thesis, review of the CIM and how the
  company actually operates, management-meeting prep, up to two calls with
  the seller or banker, a written summary of key risks and pricing points,
  and a view on shaping an LOI the seller is likely to sign
- Price: $5,000. **Launch deal: $2,500 for the first 3 buyers** (confirmed by
  Murali). Update the "spots left" count as they fill.
- If the buyer continues into full diligence, the review fee is credited in
  full against the first bill
- Full diligence: monthly fee ($12,000/month standard, $7,500 to $25,000
  depending on deal size), usually 2 to 4 months, plus an operating playbook
  delivered before closing. No success fees. After a one-month minimum it is
  month to month, cancellable with 15 days' notice.
- Launch offer window ends Feb 28, 2027
- He never reviews a deal where he represents the seller, and nothing a
  buyer shares is used on the sell side. Reviews are subject to the NDA's
  sharing permissions.
