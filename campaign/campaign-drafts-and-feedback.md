# Murali campaign: copy drafts, feedback and review

Last updated: Nov 22, 2026. Status: working draft. Nothing here has been sent to anyone.

This file keeps every version of the campaign copy in one place, with Murali's comments and a check against CLIENT.md. Word counts are approximate and include the greeting and sign-off. CLIENT.md limit for Email 1 is under 90 words.

## 1. Rules from CLIENT.md that the copy has to follow
- The only ask is a **30-minute call with Murali**. Never ask a prospect to email a CIM.
- The lead magnet ("Read the CIM Like the Person Who Wrote It", 9-area PDF) is **not sent on reply**. It is the reason to book a call.
- No price in Email 1. No guarantees of outcomes or "go/no-go" verdicts. No "free" or "lose nothing" promises.
- First person ("I"), not "we". Under 90 words. One question. No flattery, hype or jokes. Sign "Murali".
- Do not imply sellers are dishonest: a CIM can make a business look better or worse than it is.
- If the copy says Murali advises founders on the sell side, pair it with: he never reviews a deal where he represents the seller.
- Allowed claims: more than 24 CIMs written, 100+ valuations, review delivered within 5 business days (fee credited if they continue), two past-deal buyers later hired him, anonymous stories only.
- Sequence: Day 1 opener, Day 4 proof story, Day 9 path and offer, Day 16 breakup. Follow-ups go in the same thread.
