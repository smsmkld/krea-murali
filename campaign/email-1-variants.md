# Email 1: founder-dependence angle, 6 variants x 3 ICPs

Last updated: Nov 22, 2026. Draft for review. Nothing here has been sent.

One email, one offer. Only two things change per lead: the **variable** (6 variants, in the subject and opening line) and the **ICP phrases** (2 slots, by firm type). Everything else is fixed and uses Murali's approved wording.

## Fixed parts (same for everyone)

- Line 2: *Founder dependence rarely shows up in the CIM. I’ve written more than 24 CIMs and worked both sides of the table, so I know where it hides. I flag it, with other operating risks, in 5 business days, {diligence_phrase}.*
- Line 3: *Worth a short call before {next_deal_phrase}?*
- Sign-off: Murali
- Rules kept: first person, under 90 words, one question, call-only ask, no CIM request, no price, no promise, no joke.

## The 6 variants (subject + opening line)

| Variant | Subject | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Recent deal | `after {deal}` | {Name} - saw {deal} closed in {deal_month}. A pattern buyers know: the founder steps back, and pricing, key accounts and supplier terms turn out to have lived in one person’s head. |
| 2 Portfolio company | `{portfolio_company}` | {Name} - saw {portfolio_company} in your portfolio. A pattern buyers know: the founder steps back, and pricing, key accounts and supplier terms turn out to have lived in one person’s head. |
| 3 Live signal | `{live_short}` | {Name} - saw you’re {live_signal}. A pattern buyers know: the founder steps back, and pricing, key accounts and supplier terms turn out to have lived in one person’s head. |
| 4 Buy box | `{buy_box} deals` | {Name} - in founder-owned companies in your {buy_box} range, a pattern shows up: the founder steps back, and pricing, key accounts and supplier terms turn out to have lived in one person’s head. |
| 5 Founder background | `founder dependence` | {Name} - I saw you came up through {background_firm}. Even experienced buyers see this: the founder steps back, and pricing, key accounts and supplier terms turn out to have lived in one person’s head. |
| 6 Sector | `{sector} deals` | {Name} - a pattern buyers in {sector} deals know: the founder steps back, and pricing, key accounts and supplier terms turn out to have lived in one person’s head. |

## ICP slots (change by firm type)

| ICP | `{diligence_phrase}` | `{next_deal_phrase}` |
|---|---|---|
| Independent sponsor | before the LOI or full diligence | your next deal |
| Family office | before the LOI or you commit family capital | your next direct deal |
| Private equity (parked: needs Murali’s approval) | before the LOI or you commit fund capital | your next platform deal |

Private equity stays parked until Murali approves it (CLIENT.md). The slot values are ready if that changes.

## Where each variable comes from (`data/dream100-variables.csv`)

| Variable | Source |
|---|---|
| `{deal}`, `{deal_month}` | A confirmed deal from the last ~12 months, with a source link |
| `{portfolio_company}` | A company named on the firm's own portfolio page |
| `{live_signal}`, `{live_short}` | A sentence on their site saying what they are looking for now |
| `{buy_box}` | Their own stated EBITDA or revenue range |
| `{background_firm}` | A firm on the contact's LinkedIn (weakest, use carefully) |
| `{sector}` | Top sector keyword on their site (weakest) |

A firm with no variable is flagged `NO_LINE` and not written.
